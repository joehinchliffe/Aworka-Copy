Planner

Plan the jobs for each day and record the payments for jobs you have done.

How do I add jobs done or to be done on a particular date?

  1. Move the mouse into the date on the Planner that you wish to add jobs.
  2. Click
  3. A popup will appear showing a list of the jobs allowed on the date (if any), with the most overdue jobs at the top of the list.
    Due and late jobs look like: Very Overdue Overdue Due or Not Due
  4. To plan work from a particular round, select from the popup menu at the bottom.
  5. Click jobs in the list to add them to the selected date.
    On the planner, they will look like: To Do Job or Not Confirmed Job or Not Done Job (if added to a date more than a week ago)
  6. You can also drag jobs from the list to any other date. If the popup is in the way, you can move it by dragging its title bar.
  7. To quickly find a specific job, start typing part of the job address or reference into the field at the bottom of the popup. As you type, the list will reduce to those jobs containing the text you have typed. You don't have to type the full job name and you don't have to start from the beginning of the name.
  8. You can also reduce the list to show only jobs from a particular round by selecting from the popup menu at the bottom.
  9. For jobs added that are already done, click them on the Planner to mark as done and edit payment details.

How do I confirm an appointment?

  • Jobs requiring an appointment, or prior arrangement with the customer look like Not Confirmed Job
  • To confirm the appointment, click the job, then click Confirmed to change the job to a Confirmed Job and click Save

    1. Go to the Jobs & Customers page.
    2. Find and click the existing customer's name in the Customer column to view the customer's details.
    3. Click New Job to enter new job details.
    4. Set the Start Date of the job and click Add to Planner?
    5. Click Save to create the new job, and add it to the planner.

    How do I edit price or job details?

    1. Click the job on the planner
    2. In the popup, edit the Price perhaps to cover some extra work. You might also want to change the Time required for the job.
    3. Add Notes if required, perhaps to explain why the price/time has been changed.
    4. To keep the new price for future jobs, click Permanent?
    5. Click Save
    6. To view or edit any other details of the customer or job, click the Customer Name or Job Name at the top of the popup.
