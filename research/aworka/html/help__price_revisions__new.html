New Price Revision

Increase (or decrease) your Job prices by a percentage. Note that Jobs assigned to Tasks are not affected by Price Revisions: edit the task price instead.

  1. Enter the percentage Price Change to apply to your job and task prices. You can also enter a negative percentage to decrease your prices.
  2. Enter a Round to nearest value to adjust prices up or down to a more rounded figure, e.g. a price of 15.00 with a 3% increase, would give a new price of 15.45, which rounded to the nearest 0.50 would give 15.50.
  3. Enter a Minimum change figure, to prevent job and task prices being revised by small amounts, e.g. from the example above, an increase of 0.50 might be considered too small to pass on to the customer. With a minimum change figure of 1.00, the price would remain at 15.00. However, a further 3% increase would exceed the 1.00 minimum giving a new rounded price of 16.00 (calculated as 15.50 + 3% = 15.97).
  4. Select Prices older than a set period, to prevent job and task prices being updated too often, e.g. if you had just started a new job in the last few weeks, it would probably be too soon to revise its price.   5. Click Save to apply the price revision. The job list will be shown with newly prices jobs at the top. If there are any problems with the price revision, you can edit individual job prices, or return to the Prices tab to revert all new prices to their previous values.
