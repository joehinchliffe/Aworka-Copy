Reports

This page shows a monthly summary report of your jobs, payments and expenses for a specified period.

What do all these numbers mean?

  • Under Net Sales you will see the total value of jobs you have done each month in each of your rounds.
  • The Gross Income section totals up payments received each month, with Tips itemized separately. Bad Debt is payment for jobs that you have done, but will not receive payment for. Overpayments will be included in your Tips.
  • Under Net Expenses you have a summary of your expenses in each of your expense categories.
  • Your Profit Before Tax is shown, per month and in the bottom right corner, for the year. You can change the way profit is calculated by clicking Settings

How do I view a different year or period?

  • You can change the period displayed by altering the popup selections and clicking. Refresh
  • You can revert to figures for the current year by clicking Reset
  • You can change your year end date by clicking Settings

How do I print my figures?

  1. Click Print
  2. Adjust page orientation to best fit the table. Refer to the Help for your web browser.
  3. You may also want to adjust print margins and remove headers and footers. Refer to the Help for your web browser.
