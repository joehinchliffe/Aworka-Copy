Recurring Expenses

Record expenses automatically at a set frequency, for fixed monthly costs.

How do I create a recurring expense?

  1. Click the Recurring Expenses tab
  2. Click New Recurring Expense
  3. Enter an expense Category, e.g. Travel, Stationery, Tools.
  4. Enter a Description of the expense if required, e.g. for an expense in the Travel category, you may wish to record destination or mileage.
  5. Adjust the date of the expense if required
  6. Enter the amount of the expense
  7. At the bottom, set the Every to the frequency the expense occurs, and the next due date to the first expense date.
  8. Click Save
  9. If the next due date is Today or earlier, one or more expenses will be created for all appropriate dates.
  10. Subsequent expenses will be created automatically early on the next due date.

How do I edit an existing recurring expense?

  1. Locate the recurring expense to edit in the recurring expenses table and click the link in the Category column.
  2. Edit the expense details as required.
  3. Click Save
  4. If the next due date is Today or earlier, one or more expenses may be created for all appropriate dates (unless they already exist).
  5. Subsequent expenses will be created automatically on the next due date.

There are two other ways to access a recurring expense for editing:

  • Via the expenses table on the Expenses tab, by clicking Monthly next to a recurring expense that has been created automatically.
  • When editing an automatically created expense, click Monthly next to the expense date.
