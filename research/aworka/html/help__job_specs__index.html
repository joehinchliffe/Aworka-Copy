Jobs

View a list of all your jobs and customers.

How do I quickly find a customer or job?

  1. Click Reset to clear any previous filter settings.
  2. Type any part of the Customer or Job name into the field next to containing
  3. Select the Round containing the job or in
  4. Click Refresh
  5. Click the name in the Customer column, to show the full details of a customer, including address, jobs and recent activity.
  6. Click the name in the Job column, to edit the details of an existing job, including price, frequency, allowed days, job notes.

How do I create a new job?

  • If the job is for an existing customer, click their name in the Customer column to view the customer's details, then click New Job to enter new job details.
  • If the job is for a new customer, click New Customer to enter customer and job details.

How do I see who is in debt?

  1. Click Reset to clear any previous filter settings.
  2. Select Show and click Refresh
  3. The list will show customers in debt (or credit) with those in debt the longest at the top of the list.
  4. The Debt column shows the amount of debt and the date the customer first went into debt.
  5. Recent debts are shown in yellow through to red for debts older than a month.
  6. Customers in credit are shown at the end of the list in green.

How do I review my prices?

  1. Click Reset to clear any previous filter settings.
  2. Select Show and click Refresh
  3. A new Price Revised column is displayed. For new prices, the previous job price is shown, which is useful to verify pricing changes. For old prices, the date the price was last revised is shown (in red if this date is more than a year ago).
  4. The list will show newly updated prices (if any) at the top of the list, in price order, followed by all others jobs in Price Revised date order, with the oldest prices first.
  5. To revise job prices, or revert the previous Price Revision click the Prices tab.

How do I handle late payment from a customer?

  1. Select Show or use the containing field to find the customer.
  2. Click the amount in the Debt column for the customer.
  3. Check and edit the Paid amount.
  4. If the customer tipped, enter the total including tip into the Paid field, click inc Tip? then check and edit the Tip amount.
  5. Click Save

How do I reactivate a suspended job?

  1. Click Reset to clear any previous filter settings.
  2. Select Show
  3. Click the name in the Job column, to edit the job details.
  4. Click Suspend? to mark the job as no longer suspended.
  5. Click Save

How do I update multiple jobs and/or customers at the same time?

  1. Click the in the first column of the table, for all the jobs you want to edit together. You can go to other pages and continue selecting jobs.
  2. When multiple jobs are selected an Edit 5 Jobs button appears at the bottom left of table (the number shows the total number of selected jobs).
  3. To quickly select or deselect all the jobs currently filtered, click the in the table header. Note that this affects jobs on all pages, not just those visible on the screen.
  4. To view only the currently selected jobs, ensure the filter is Reset then select Show and click Refresh
  5. Click the Edit 5 Jobs to edit all the selected jobs together.
