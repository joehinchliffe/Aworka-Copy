Debts

View a list of all your customer debts and/or credits.

How do I mark a debt as paid?

  1. Click the the debt balance to show the payment popup.
  2. Edit the payment details.
  3. Click Save

How do I print a debt list?

  1. Select the debts you wish to include using the options next to Show
    For a printed debt list used for door-to-door collection, typical settings might be Show by
  2. Click Refresh
  3. Click Print... to display a printable debt list will be displayed.
  4. Click Print

How do I write off a debt?

  1. Click the customer's name to go the customer's overview page.
  2. Click the Bad Debt button to show the bad debt popup.
  3. Enter the amount of debt you wish to write off.
  4. Click Save
  5. The bad debt will appear in the customer's activity table, where it can be edited/deleted like other payments.

How do I quickly find a debt?

  1. Click Reset to clear any previous filter settings.
  2. Type any part of the Customer or Job name into the field next to containing
  3. Select the Round containing the job or in
  4. Click Refresh
