New Customer

Create a job for a new customer.

Edit Customer Details

  1. Enter the customer's Name. You need to enter at least one of Title, First Name(s) and Last Name.
  2. For commercial customers, enter the Company name.
  3. Enter the customer's address. This is optional, unless the job Location is set to Customer Address or this is an invoice customer.
  4. Enter up to three Phone numbers, e.g. home, work and/or mobile/cell.
  5. Enter an email address.
  6. If this customer does not pay by invoice, select Cash. Otherwise, choose how you would like to send invoices to this customer: Email or Paper, how often invoices should be sent and how long the customer has to pay
  7. Enter Notes about this customer, e.g. names of other people you may deal with.
  8. If you have worked for this customer previously and they owe or are owed money, you can enter their Starting Balance and date. if the customer is in debt, enter a negative balance.

Edit Job Location, Reference and Round

  1. Choose the job's Location. If job is at the same address as the customer, choose Customer Address. If the job is at an alternative address, e.g. a commercial customer with multiple sites, Choose Other Address and extra fields will appear to allow entry of the job address. If job location isn't applicable, e.g. the job is at your own premises, choose Not Applicable.
  2. Enter a Reference for the job. If not specified, the job will be identified on the Planner and elsewhere by the first line of the job's address. The Reference must be specified if the job Location is Not Applicable.
  3. If you would like to group your jobs into rounds, enter the name of the New Round that will contain this job. Rounds are useful for grouping jobs normally done together, perhaps because they are near to each other.

Select Task or Edit Job Description, Time and Price

  1. To define common tasks with a standard set of prices, click the Prices tab.
  2. Enter a Description of the job (if needed) and any extra Notes. Only the Description is shown on invoices, so Notes can be used for private information.
  3. Set the Time required for the job by picking values from the hours and minutes popup menus. Be sure to include travel time (if any). This is used to se the height of the job on the Planner giving a visual indication of the total amount of work on each day.
  4. Enter the normal Price for the job.

Edit Job Appointments, Job frequency, Allowed days and Next due date

  1. Choose a value for Appointments to indicate how the job should be scheduled.
  2. For regular jobs, you can specify the Job Frequency. This used to determine when the job is next due after it has been done.
  3. Where the customer wants the job done only on certain days of the week, pick the Allowed Days as required.
  4. Set the Next Due Date for the job and optionally click Add to Planner? to add this job to the Planner on the Next Due Date specified.
  5. Finally, click Save to create the job.
